About this site

Coverage
Lectures 09 to 17 and textbook chapter 2 (2.3, 2.6.1 to 2.6.2) and chapter 3 (3.1 to 3.4).
Not covered here
The pthreads lectures (01 to 08). Lectures 12 and 13 recap the pthreads versions where they compare them.
Course code
mpi_hello.c, mpi_hello2.c, mpi_SendRec.c, mpi_Bcast.c and heatDiffusionMpi.c, all compiled and run. mpi_odd_even.c and arrAdd.py were not in the project files, so those use the slide code.
How outputs were made
MPICH for lectures 09 to 11, Open MPI for the heat program, Warp 1.16 on the CPU for the GPU example.
Sources
The CIS*3090 slides and An Introduction to Parallel Programming (Pacheco and Malensek, 2nd ed.). Lecture 13 also uses Grama et al. for the neighbour computation.
